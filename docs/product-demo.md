# Demo walkthrough (about five minutes, no keys)

Everything in the demo workspace is **synthetic**: fictional companies, people and `fixture://` documents.

1. **Enter demo** from the sign-in page. The library shows two graphs with real counts and a live mini-map.
2. Open **Series B product leaders**. Solid nodes are verified, dashed ones provisional. The amber marker on
   *Quillon Data* means sources disagree.
3. Click *Quillon Data*. The inspector shows each attribute with its status, dates and sources. Open **HQ location**
   to see the supporting and the contradicting quote. Click the magnifier beside a connection to inspect an edge.
4. Press **Start research → Start simulation**. Provisional nodes bloom into the map, the area being investigated
   pulses, and a live ticker shows what the agents are doing. Refresh the page mid-run: nothing is lost.
5. Open **Research**: plan, searches, sources read, verification outcomes, budget meters, pause/cancel, and the
   agent's open questions.
6. Open **Review**. Approve an entity, reject another (it stays in *Decided* as an audit record), look at the
   *possible duplicate* (two people named Daniel Okafor) and the dated title change.
7. Open **Changes**. A monitor found that Elena Vasquez's title changed, with before/after evidence. Press
   **Check now** on another monitor: an unchanged source produces no alert.
8. Drag the **time machine** at the bottom of the canvas, or press play, to see the graph as it was known earlier.
9. **⌘K** opens the command palette. **New graph** drafts an editable charter from a prompt.
